enum BipedalLocomotion::RobotInterface::ControlMode
strong

ControlMode contains the supported control modes of a joint.

Note
The units refer to revolute joints. The references are set with IRobotControl::setReferences().
The values are explicit since they are stored in the logs (e.g., the joints_state::control_modes channel of the YarpRobotLoggerDevice): do not change them.
Enumerator
Position 

The joint reaches the desired position [rad] following a trajectory generated by the low-level controller.

Use it for point-to-point motions.

PositionDirect 

The desired position [rad] is tracked directly, without any trajectory.

The reference has to be streamed at every control cycle.

Velocity 

The joint tracks the desired velocity [rad/s].

Torque 

The joint tracks the desired torque [Nm].

PWM 

The desired PWM duty cycle is applied to the motor.

Current 

The motor tracks the desired current [A].

Idle 

The joint is not controlled and the motor is not powered.

No reference is accepted.

ForceIdle 

Like Idle, but it also acknowledges the hardware fault of the joint, if any.

It is the only mode accepted by a joint in HardwareFault. Once set, the joint is reported as Idle, or as HardwareFault while the fault persists.

HardwareFault 

The joint is in hardware fault and it is not controlled.

This mode can only be read: the joint leaves it after ForceIdle, when the fault is cleared.

Unknown 

The control mode is not available or not supported.
